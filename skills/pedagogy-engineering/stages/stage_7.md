# Stage 7: Depth and Complexity

Determine exactly how deeply and proficiently each objective must be mastered, establishing realistic expectations of cognitive rigor.

---

## 1. Depth Taxonomies

Calibrate each objective according to explicit performance depth levels:

1. **Recognition**: Identifying terms, components, or symbols among given choices.
2. **Recall**: Reproducing definitions, formulas, or facts from memory without cues.
3. **Explanation**: Explaining causal mechanisms, principles, or logic in learner's own words.
4. **Interpretation**: Translating representations (e.g. graph to equation, schematic to wiring, code to flow).
5. **Guided Application**: Executing procedures with prompts, templates, or partial solutions.
6. **Independent Application**: Executing standard procedures autonomously in familiar contexts.
7. **Analysis**: Deconstructing unscripted problems, isolating root causes, diagnosing anomalies.
8. **Creation**: Synthesizing elements into a novel solution, design, circuit, or program.
9. **Evaluation**: Assessing competing options or solutions against formal criteria and justifying trade-offs.
10. **Fluent Performance**: Executing with high speed, low cognitive friction, and automaticity.

---

## 2. The Four Dimension Calibration

For each objective (`LO-xx`), explicitly specify:

* **Expected Cognitive Depth**: Chosen from the taxonomy above.
* **Problem Complexity**: Low (isolated single-variable), Medium (multi-step with confounding factors), or High (ambiguous, non-linear, competing constraints).
* **Degree of Independence**: Full scaffolding, faded assistance, peer collaboration, or completely unassisted.
* **Acceptable Performance Conditions**: Allowed tools (calculators, cheat sheets, syntax hints), time limits, and environmental conditions.

> [!TIP]
> **Avoid Over-Demanding Depth**: Never calibrate an objective to "Evaluation" or "Creation" if the overarching learning goal only calls for "Guided Application" or "Explanation".

---

