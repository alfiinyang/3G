# Stage 5: Knowledge and Skill Structure

Deconstruct the cognitive and practical substance required to achieve the learning objectives into structured knowledge types.

---

## 1. Knowledge Taxonomies

Classify all required learning elements into the four primary categories:

### 1.1 Declarative Knowledge (Knowing "What")
* Facts, terminology, notations, formulas, definitions, attributes, rules, and domain-specific information.
* *Examples*: Keyword syntax, electrical component names, unit definitions, safety codes.

### 1.2 Conceptual Knowledge (Knowing "Why")
* Mental models, principles, categorizations, interrelationships, causal systems, and governing theories.
* *Examples*: Voltage as potential difference, scope and variable lifetime in programming, supply and demand dynamics.

### 1.3 Procedural Knowledge (Knowing "How")
* Step-by-step algorithms, techniques, practical execution methods, workflows, and action sequences.
* *Examples*: Debugging a breakpoint trace, wiring a three-way switch, factoring a quadratic equation.

### 1.4 Strategic / Metacognitive Knowledge (Knowing "When & Which")
* Decision rules, criteria for selecting methods, troubleshooting heuristics, self-monitoring, and strategy adaptation.
* *Examples*: Deciding whether to use a hash table vs array, troubleshooting an intermittent network drop, planning an essay structure.

---

## 2. Objective Mapping Rule

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - Include **only** the categories genuinely required by the diagnosed learning problem.
> - **Every single content element** must be mapped to at least one Learning Objective (`LO-xx`). Orphaned knowledge without an objective must be removed.
