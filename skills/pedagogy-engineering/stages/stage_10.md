# Stage 10: Learning Activities & Activity Specification

Specify the concrete cognitive and practical activities learners must execute in order to master each learning objective.

---

## 1. Principles of Learning Activities

1. **Active Performance**: Avoid passive exposition (reading text, watching video) unless immediately followed by active application. The learner must execute the target cognitive or physical behavior.
2. **Objective Coverage**: Every learning objective (`LO-xx`) must be addressed by at least one dedicated learning activity.
3. **Valid Action Modes**:
   - *Observation & Analysis*: Observe patterns, identify flaws, compare cases.
   - *Exploration & Prediction*: Formulate hypotheses, predict outcomes, manipulate variables.
   - *Construction & Solution*: Solve equations, construct circuits, write logic, assemble mechanisms.
   - *Troubleshooting & Reflection*: Diagnose errors, isolate failure points, reflect on strategy.

---

## 2. The 10-Point Activity Specification

For every major activity, specify all 10 pedagogical fields:

1. **Activity ID**: Unique identifier (e.g., `ACT-01`, `ACT-02`).
2. **Objective Addressed**: Exact mapping to `LO-xx`.
3. **Learner Action**: What the learner physically/cognitively performs.
4. **Knowledge/Skill Exercised**: Specific declarative, conceptual, or procedural items utilized.
5. **Context**: Scenario or problem instance in which the action occurs.
6. **Prerequisites**: Necessary prior knowledge or preceding activity.
7. **Expected Performance**: What constitutes competent execution.
8. **Support Required**: Level of scaffolding, hints, or references available.
9. **Feedback Required**: Immediate feedback provided during or after the activity.
10. **Evidence Generated**: Observable artifact or output produced that proves mastery.

---
