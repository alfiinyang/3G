# Stage 9: Learning Scenario

Define the contextual situation in which learners apply target knowledge and skills to resolve meaningful challenges.

---

## 1. Pedagogical Scenario vs. Game Narrative

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strict Prohibition**:
> The learning scenario is **strictly pedagogical**, NOT game fiction or narrative worldbuilding.
> - **DO NOT PRESCRIBE**: Characters, character backstories, narrative lore, visual aesthetics, fantasy worlds, cinematic cutscenes, or specific game mechanics.
> - **DO PRESCRIBE**: Real-world or functional context, professional/operational role, authentic problem, knowledge deployed, action taken, and causal consequence.

---

## 2. The Six Essential Elements

Every learning scenario must define:

1. **Contextual Situation**: The operational environment or domain context in which the problem arises (e.g., an automated logistics warehouse experiencing sorting delays; an emergency clinic during triage).
2. **Learner Role**: The functional capacity in which the learner operates (e.g., Junior Systems Analyst, Electrical Inspector, Financial Auditor).
3. **Authentic Problem**: The operational failure, challenge, or discrepancy that demands resolution.
4. **Required Knowledge & Skills**: The specific concepts and procedures that must be deployed.
5. **Target Learner Action**: What the learner actually decides, calculates, diagnoses, or executes.
6. **Meaningful Consequence**: Realistic feedback or outcome resulting from valid vs invalid learner actions (e.g. system throughput normalizes vs conveyor jam worsens).

---

## 3. Required Output Format

```markdown
### 9. Learning Scenario
- **Operational Situation**: [Authentic setting where the challenge occurs]
- **Learner Role**: [Functional role assumed by the learner]
- **Authentic Problem**: [Discrepancy, failure, or challenge demanding action]
- **Required Knowledge / Skill**: [Core principles applied]
- **Target Learner Action**: [Decision, execution, diagnosis, or evaluation]
- **Meaningful Consequences**:
  - *Correct Execution*: [Realistic domain outcome indicating success]
  - *Flawed / Suboptimal Execution*: [Realistic domain failure illustrating the flaw]
```
