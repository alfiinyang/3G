# Stage 15: Pedagogical Approach

Select, synthesize, and justify the instructional paradigm(s) best suited to the diagnosed learning problem.

---

## 1. Candidate Pedagogical Paradigms

Choose or combine established frameworks based on educational efficacy:

* **Direct Instruction**: High-structure, explicit modeling, and systematic practice (ideal for foundational syntax, safety rules, formulas).
* **Inquiry-Based Learning**: Question-driven exploration, testing hypotheses, and drawing conclusions from data.
* **Problem-Based Learning (PBL)**: Anchored in complex, unscripted real-world challenges driving the need to learn.
* **Experiential Learning (Kolb)**: Concrete experience $\to$ reflective observation $\to$ abstract conceptualization $\to$ active experimentation.
* **Mastery Learning**: Non-linear progression requiring demonstrated competency (e.g. 90%+) before unlocking subsequent stages.
* **Deliberate Practice (Ericsson)**: Repetitive, focused practice targeting specific micro-skills with immediate corrective feedback.
* **Simulation-Based Learning**: Immersive execution within a high-fidelity dynamic causal model.
* **Scenario-Based Learning**: Decision-tree navigation through realistic narrative consequences.

---

## 2. Mandatory Directives

> [!CAUTION]
> **No Faddish Selection**:
> Never choose an instructional framework simply because it sounds modern, fashionable, or entertaining. The choice must be rigorously defended against the diagnosed cognitive gap.

1. **Defend the Fit**: Prove why this approach directly bridges the diagnosed deficit from Stage 2.
2. **Map to Objectives**: Identify which specific `LO-xx` objectives are governed by this approach.
3. **Trace Forward**: Pass the selected pedagogical approach as a direct mandatory input into the subsequent **Learning Experience Requirements (Stage 16)** and **Learning Environment Requirements (Stage 17)**.
