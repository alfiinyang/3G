# Stage 13: Evidence of Learning

Establish unambiguous assessment metrics to prove that target capabilities have been acquired, isolating genuine learning from game mechanics.

---

## 1. Disentangling Learning from Game Success

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strict Prohibition**:
> Unrelated in-game accomplishments (e.g., reaching a high score, collecting coins, fast twitch reflexes, completing a level through trial-and-error guessing) **must never** be accepted as evidence of learning.
> 
> Evidence must measure the observable deployment of the target knowledge, reasoning, or skill.

---

## 2. The Four Evidence Pillars

For every Learning Objective (`LO-xx`), explicitly define:

1. **Target Performance**: The exact observable task the learner must complete.
2. **Observable Evidence**: The concrete output, telemetry, artifact, code, or diagnostic response recorded.
3. **Success Criteria**: Rubric, accuracy benchmark, error tolerance, or threshold that qualifies as mastery.
4. **Assessment Conditions**: Context, time constraints, allowed references, and absence of scaffolding.

---

## 3. Required Output Format

```markdown

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
```
