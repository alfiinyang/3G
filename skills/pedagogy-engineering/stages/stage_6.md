# Stage 6: Content Scope

Establish rigorous, unambiguous instructional boundaries to ensure focus and prevent cognitive overload.

---

## 1. Scope Classifications

For the learning objectives defined in Stage 4, classify all related domain content into five explicit tiers:

### 1.1 Core Content (Mandatory)
* Indispensable topics, principles, and procedures without which the learning goal cannot be achieved.
* Must be mastered by every learner.

### 1.2 Supporting Content (Enabling)
* Contextual information, examples, background context, or minor secondary facts that facilitate understanding of the core content.

### 1.3 Optional / Extension Content (Enrichment)
* Advanced nuances, optional edge cases, or stretch challenges that may be offered to fast or advanced learners, but are not required for goal achievement.

### 1.4 Prerequisite Content (Assumed Prior)
* Concepts or skills learners are expected to already know upon entering. Must not be taught from scratch, though baseline activation or diagnostic checks may be included.

### 1.5 Excluded Content (Explicit Out-of-Scope)
* Related domain topics, advanced edge cases, heavy mathematical proofs, or tangential details that are explicitly forbidden from the plan to prevent scope creep.

---

## 2. Scope Containment Rules

> [!CAUTION]
> - **Zero Hidden Scope**: Material listed as excluded or out-of-scope must **never** appear as an implicit prerequisite or hidden challenge in later activities, assessments, or game requirements.
> - **Strict Proportionality**: The volume of core content must be realistic for the audience and available learning time defined in Stage 0.
