# Stage 16: Learning Experience Requirements

Determine what the learner needs to **experience** educationally in order for the intended learning process to succeed.

---

## 1. Mandatory Directives

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Educational Function over Game Mechanics**:
> Specify what the learner needs to encounter, feel, and exercise cognitively—**NOT** what the game should aesthetically look like or what low-level button mechanics to code.
> - **DO NOT PRESCRIBE**: Visual art style, narrative lore, UI wireframes, minigames, or thematic packaging.
> - **DO PRESCRIBE**: Learner agency, psychological safety, cognitive dissonance, consequence-driven pressure, exploration boundaries, and progressive responsibility.

Answer this core question:
> **"What kind of experience must the learner undergo for the intended learning process to achieve its objectives?"**

Derive every experience requirement directly from:
- Learning Objectives (Stage 4)
- Learning Activities (Stage 10)
- Learning Progression & Scaffolding (Stages 8 & 11)
- Feedback & Transfer Requirements (Stages 12 & 14)
- Pedagogical Approach (Stage 15)

---

## 2. Core Experience Dimensions to Evaluate

Select and specify only those dimensions necessary for the pedagogical goal:

* **Learner Agency**: Degree of autonomy the learner has to choose strategies, sequences, or methods.
* **Controlled Discovery / Exploration**: Freedom to probe the problem space and test assumptions before receiving formal instruction.
* **Active Experimentation**: Opportunities to hypothesize, test variables, and witness causal reactions.
* **Authentic Uncertainty**: Exposure to realistic ambiguity, imperfect data, or competing priorities without artificially trivializing the task.
* **Consequence-Driven Learning**: Encountering realistic domain outcomes (successes and setbacks) that reinforce understanding.
* **Progressive Responsibility**: Moving from heavily guided execution to full operational ownership.
* **Deliberate Practice / Repetition**: Experiencing focused, varied micro-challenges with low latency corrective feedback.
* **Collaborative Interaction**: Co-constructing solutions, negotiating trade-offs, or peer review (if relevant).
* **Structured Reflection**: Pausing to debrief, review logs, and analyze the causal chain of decisions.

---

For every major experience requirement, specify all six fields:

```markdown

- **Expected Learner Behavior**: [Observable cognitive or behavioral response, e.g., actively probing symptoms before modifying settings]
- **Progression Implications**: [How this experience evolves across progression stages I through IV]
- **Game-Design Implication**: [Direct functional directive for Stage 18, e.g., must simulate real-time fault propagation rather than instant game-over]
```
